How the Recovery System Works

Four stages ensure seamless, fraud-resistant campus recoveries.

STAGE 01
1. Private Submission

Student submits a report privately. Details like serial numbers, distinctive marks, and phone numbers are stored confidentially.

STAGE 02
2. AI Matching Engine

Modular Scikit-Learn TF-IDF vectors evaluate textual similarity, category compatibility, brand tokens, and campus location proximity.

STAGE 03
3. Admin Verification

Campus officers inspect suggested matches, conduct secure student contact attempts, and approve or request additional evidence.

STAGE 04
4. Verified Hand-off

Owner inspects the physical item at the Campus Help Desk, verifies lock screen/credentials, and an immutable recovery record is created.
