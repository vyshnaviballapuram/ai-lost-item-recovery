Campus Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Confidentiality Guarantee for Lost and Found Belongings

1. Zero Automatic Publication

To prevent opportunistic fraud, theft, or stalking, no report is ever published immediately upon submission. Every report is stored securely in the database with a PENDING status. Public listings show only administrator-approved reports and strip confidential markings.

2. Protection of Distinctive Marks & Identifiers

When submitting a report, fields such as serial numbers, unique scratches, internal contents, lock screen patterns, and engraving are marked as strictly confidential. These values are never visible in public listings and are accessible only to verified campus security officers to evaluate ownership claims.

3. Contact Information Privacy

Students' personal phone numbers and private email addresses are never displayed publicly. Authorized administrators contact reporters directly through verified channels, and contact logs are audited to maintain student safety.

4. AI Match Suggestions Privacy

AI-generated potential matches are treated as confidential operational intelligence. Ordinary users cannot see matches involving unapproved reports.
